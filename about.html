Why we started

Most small businesses end up with three or four different suppliers. A freelancer built the website. Someone else runs the ads. A cousin edits the videos. Nobody owns the result, and when performance drops, everyone points at someone else.

We started MMU IT Solutions in 2026 because we kept seeing the same pattern. The individual pieces were fine. The joins between them were where everything fell apart. A great ad sending traffic to a slow page. A good website with nobody following up on the enquiries it generated.

So we do all five under one roof. Not because we want to sell you more, but because when one team owns the ad, the page, the follow-up and the video, there is nowhere for the blame to go.

How we work

  • We tell you when the answer is no. If your budget is too small for ads to work, we will say so rather than take the money and produce a bad result.
  • You own everything. Ad accounts, domains, hosting, workflows and raw video files stay in your name. We never hold anything hostage.
  • Monthly, not annual. Ongoing work runs month to month. If we are not earning it, you should be able to leave without a penalty clause.
  • Real numbers only. Reports show what happened, including the months that went badly.
