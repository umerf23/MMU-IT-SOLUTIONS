Website Development questions

Do you use WordPress?

Only when a client genuinely needs it. For most marketing sites we build static sites which are faster, cheaper to host and far harder to hack.

Who owns the domain and hosting?

You do. Everything is registered in your name and we simply get access. If we part ways, nothing switches off.

Can you redesign my existing site?

Yes. We start with an audit of what is currently costing you conversions, then decide whether to rebuild or repair.

How long does a build take?

A landing page takes about five working days. A six page site takes two to three weeks, mostly depending on how quickly content comes back from you.
