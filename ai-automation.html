Automate the admin nobody enjoys

Workflows that quietly handle the copying, pasting, chasing and reporting your team currently does by hand.

AI Automation questions

Will this replace my staff?

Usually it replaces the boring part of their job rather than the job. The point is to stop paying skilled people to copy data between tabs.

What tools do you build on?

Whatever fits, commonly n8n, Make or direct API integrations. We pick based on your existing stack and budget, not on which platform pays a referral fee.

What happens if something breaks?

Alerts fire immediately rather than failing silently. If you are on a support plan, we fix it. If not, the documentation explains how to.

Is my data safe?

Data stays in your accounts. We work with the minimum access needed and remove our access when the project ends.
