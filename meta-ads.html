Meta Ads that bring in enquiries

Facebook and Instagram campaigns judged on cost per enquiry, not on likes, reach or any other number that does not pay your rent.

The problem we are solving. Most small business ad accounts fail for the same three reasons: the creative looks like an advert, the audience is too broad, and nobody is tracking what happens after the click. Boosting a post is not a campaign.

What is included

  • Full account audit before we touch anything
  • Campaign structure built around one clear objective
  • Pixel and Conversions API set up so tracking survives iOS privacy limits
  • Six creative variations refreshed every month
  • Systematic audience and hook testing rather than guesswork
  • Fortnightly report showing spend, cost per lead and what we changed

How it works

STEP 01

Audit

We look at your existing account, past campaigns and current tracking. You get the findings whether or not you hire us.

STEP 02

Setup

Pixel, Conversions API, conversion events and campaign structure. This is the part most accounts get wrong.

STEP 03

Test

Small budgets across several hooks and audiences to find what works before we spend real money.

STEP 04

Scale

Budget moves to the winners. Losers get switched off quickly rather than nursed along.

Meta Ads questions

What is the minimum ad spend?

We recommend at least Rs 75,000 a month paid to Meta. Below that there is not enough data to test properly, and you end up paying us to guess.

Do you need access to my ad account?

Yes, as a partner on your existing Business Manager. We never ask you to create the account under our name, because it should stay yours.

How long before I see results?

The learning phase usually takes two to three weeks. Judging a campaign in its first week is the most common mistake we see.

What if the ads do not work?

Then we tell you, and we look at why. Sometimes the ads are fine and the landing page or the follow-up is the actual problem.
